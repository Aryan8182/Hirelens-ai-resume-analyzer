Job Title: Machine Learning & AI Engineer

- Collaborate with software engineers using Git, GitHub, and Agile methodologies.

Required Technical Skills:

- ML/DL Frameworks: TensorFlow, PyTorch, Scikit-learn, SpaCy, HuggingFace Transformers
- AI Concepts: Deep Learning, NLP, Computer Vision, RAG, Vector Databases (ChromaDB/Pinecone)

- Cloud & DevOps: AWS, Docker, Kubernetes, Git, REST APIs
- Soft Skills: Problem Solving, Teamwork, Critical Thinking
